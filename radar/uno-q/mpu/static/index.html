Buzzer

1 ft is 100 Hz. Each half foot adds 400 Hz, up to 5000 Hz.

Pitch 2000 Hz

The tone updates every 0.2 s from the latest detection. 1 ft is 100 Hz, 1.5 ft is 500 Hz, 2 ft is 900 Hz, and 2.5 ft is 1300 Hz. The tone stays at 5000 Hz past about 7 ft. Nothing in view keeps the buzzer quiet.
